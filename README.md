# Title: Ricky The Rookie
# Genre: Shooter
## Sub Genre: 2D Airplane Combat Shooter

## Core Idea:
The game follows Ricky, a Rookie pilot, as he tries to prove his worth by becoming as good as the Ace Pilots who made fun of him and even tried to kill him to prove a point. The idea is that Ricky must battle through 3 missions and the conclusion by fighting a final boss at the end that has a shocking plot twist to how Ricky the Rookie might potentially meet his doom. However, even though Ricky must face increasing difficulty, in the end, he will do his best to prove he is an Ace Pilot and complete his mission.

## Mechanics:
- Player controls a fighter jet
- 2D Movement (up, down, left, right)
-  Shooting System (pressing space to fire bullets)
-  Enemy System (missile spawning)
-  Collision detection (Player vs Missile)
-  Dodging incoming missiles
-  Health System for Player
-  Increasingly difficult across levels
-  PowerUps to Jet

## Win/Loss Conditions:
If Ricky the Rookie’s Jet gets hit too many times (3 times), he will explode and lose. The winning condition is to complete the 3 levels and waves of enemies, to get to the last stage with the final boss, and once beaten, you make it to the end and win the game. There is an option for endless mode after defeating the boss. 
 
## UI Plan:
The UI will comprise a Scoring system to save progress similar to arcades.  Customization will also be an option for the player (jet color). Implementation also includes a title screen, start game, pause game, settings, and restart menu. A Win or Lose Screen also appears based on the condition of whether you die or beat the game.
